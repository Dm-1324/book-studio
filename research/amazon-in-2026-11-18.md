## What it means
- Indian buyers pay ₹119-₹420 for puzzle books. US-printed KDP books land at ₹666-₹1,471 and barely sell
  (ranks 150k+ even with hundreds of US reviews).
- KDP does not print paperbacks in India; India print = local POD (Pothi, Notion Press) later.
- Winners in India: cheap B&W multi-puzzle books, smaller trims (6x9 / 7.5x9.25 in), "for seniors / parents /
  grandparents / retirement gift" titles, Level 1 / Level 2 series, 2-book combo packs.
- Trending: murder-mystery logic puzzles in full colour (Murdoku #63 overall, Murdle, Poirot, Sherlock).
- Bird word search on Amazon.in: only imported US books, no local competition, but also no proven demand.
- Listings use long keyword titles ("... - Book for Father, Mother, Grandparents - Retirement Gift") and a
  "Generic Name" field stuffed with search terms.
